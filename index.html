A LITTLE ABOUT THE BRAND

It's time to make
room for real talk.

Between You & Me is an educational personal brand focused on sex education, intimacy, sexual health awareness, and healthy relationships. We believe people deserve information that is understandable, respectful, and free from judgment.

Our goal is to make important conversations feel more approachable, challenge misinformation, and encourage informed decisions. This is a learning space — not a substitute for personalized medical advice.
